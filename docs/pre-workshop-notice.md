# Pre-workshop notice (for InTELECT to send to participants)

**Subject:** 一盏课 One Cup, One Lesson: please prepare before Wednesday 11 November

Dear colleagues,

Thank you for registering for the InTELECT & LC workshop **Creating Interactive Lessons for Online Learning Using Artificial Intelligence and Microsoft Learning Zone for Canvas Integration**.

**Date:** Wednesday, 11 November 2026
**Time:** 2:00 PM to 4:00 PM
**Venue:** InTELECT Smart Classroom (Room 4), Chancellor Hall, UBD

Everything for the session is on one page, which will open step by step during the workshop:
**https://taykokjun-88.github.io/sharingsession/**

So that we can spend our time building rather than installing, please prepare the following before you come:

1. **Bring your own laptop.** A Windows laptop is preferred.
2. **Install Microsoft Learning Zone** from learningzone.microsoft.com (Windows only) and sign in with your UBD Microsoft account. A normal laptop can make lessons of up to 10 slides; a Copilot+ PC has no limit.
3. **Have an AI account ready** for Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini, and check that you can log in. A free account is fine. The demonstration will use Claude (claude.ai).
4. **Have a Google account** you can use to create a Google Site.
5. **Bring some course material as a Word or PDF file**: notes, a reading or a set of questions from one of your modules. A short topic works best.
6. **Check your Canvas access**: you should be able to edit a Page in at least one course.

By the end of the afternoon, each of you will have at least one interactive lesson in Canvas.

We look forward to seeing you.
